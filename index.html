About Jay

Jay Mudholkar - Leader, Innovator, Advocate

Jay Mudholkar brings a unique blend of business innovation, public service, and political leadership to Connecticut's 4th District. After a distinguished career building startups and advising national campaigns, Jay understands the challenges facing our state and is committed to crafting solutions that work for all.

As the founding COO and Head of Product at a stealth startup, Jay led product development for innovative technology solutions. His experience as a Consultant and Chief of Staff for major political campaigns has given him insight into both public policy and electoral strategy.

Jay's vision focuses on strengthening Connecticut's economy, supporting families, and ensuring our state remains competitive in the global marketplace. He believes in working across party lines to achieve practical results for working families.
